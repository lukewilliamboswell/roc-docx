[.lead]
roc-docx reads and writes Word documents (`.docx`) in pure Roc, including
their comments and tracked changes. This manual is for application authors
who use the package and for contributors who change it.

ifdef::backend-html5[]
https://github.com/lukewilliamboswell/roc-docx[View roc-docx on GitHub].
The link:api/index.html[API reference] lists every module, type and
function, generated by `roc docs` from the package's own documentation.
endif::[]

.How to use this manual
****
Deciding whether to use roc-docx:: <<what-roc-docx-is>> says what the package
does, who it is for and what it leaves out.
Using it for the first time:: <<read-and-write-the-parts-of-a-document>>
opens a document, lists what is inside it and writes it to a new package;
<<read-a-documents-text>> reads its body's text, blocks, formatting and styles,
its sections, headers and footers, and its properties;
<<read-a-documents-comments>> reads its comments and the text they are about,
adds, replies to, resolves and removes them, and writes them back as threads;
<<read-a-documents-tracked-changes>> reads its tracked changes, its text
with every change accepted or rejected, and whether it tracks changes,
accepts and rejects changes, edits its text silently or as tracked changes,
turns tracking on or off, and writes every change back;
<<write-a-document>> writes the body from that model, in one canonical form;
<<generate-a-document>> starts a new document with a minimal set of styles
and lists that Word and LibreOffice recognise, and builds its content.
Reading documents you did not write:: <<read-untrusted-and-imperfect-documents>>
bounds what a document may consume, and lists what reading fails with,
what it reports and what it repairs.
Understanding the design:: <<architecture>> is the enduring design: the
pipeline, the document model, fidelity rules, the oracles and the tests.
Changing the package:: <<contribute-to-roc-docx>> covers the checks, the
style, the performance ratchets and how documentation is kept.
Upgrading:: <<release-notes>> record each release's changes, package URL and
tested compiler.
****
