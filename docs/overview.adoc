= What roc-docx is

roc-docx is a pure Roc package for Word documents in the Office Open XML
format, the `.docx` files that Microsoft Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs
produce. It is written for applications that handle business documents:
contracts, agreements, letters, reports, minutes and the review cycles around
them, where the comments and tracked changes are as important as the text.

== Three jobs

Read:: Open a document another tool produced and recover its content
faithfully: text, structure, basic formatting, tables, comments with their
authors and dates, and every tracked change.
Modify:: Change a document and write it back without losing anything the
application did not touch: add or resolve comments, accept or reject
revisions, record new edits as tracked changes, fill in content.
Generate:: Produce new documents from application data that open cleanly in
Word and LibreOffice: headings, lists, tables, headers and footers, page
numbers, comments and tracked changes.

roc-docx understands a document as its author structured it, not as a page
image. It does not lay out, paginate, render or convert to other formats, and
it never contacts the network. The
xref:architecture.adoc#scope-and-claims[scope and claims] section of the
architecture lists the supported content and the constructs that are preserved
but not modelled.

== What you can rely on

Every capability roc-docx claims is backed by evidence from independent
oracles: the ECMA-376 schemas validate every part it writes, LibreOffice reads
what it writes and agrees on what it reads, and pandoc produces a regenerable
fixture corpus with known content. The package is pure Roc with pure Roc
dependencies, it never crashes on any input, it bounds the resources a
document may consume, and the same document always serializes to the same
bytes.

The package is under development and claims no document capability yet. What
it can do so far is open a package, list its parts, read their XML, and
write a document back with its parts and XML trees kept, which
xref:getting-started.adoc#read-and-write-the-parts-of-a-document[the next chapter]
shows, and read the text of its body as paragraphs and runs, which
xref:reading-text.adoc#read-a-documents-text[the chapter after it] shows.
